# Public research syndication check | 2026-11-19

Decision: No qualifying channel verified. A publisher-linked public RSS/Atom channel covering the specified research **and** documented permission to show its headlines and links on another site were both required. No adapter. This is not a claim that no feed endpoint exists.

- **HVS research publications / market snapshots.** The [publication directory](https://hvs.com/publications/) identifies recent research separately from its footer's News RSS Feed. The [official news RSS](https://hvs.com/news/rss/rss.aspx) describes project work, research and other news; occasional research announcements do not make it a research feed. [HVS terms](https://hvs.com/aboutHVS/termsofuse) require express permission for publication or redistribution. No research-only syndication invitation was located.
- **Skift Research reports / press notes.** [Skift terms](https://skift.com/terms/) welcome ordinary hyperlinks but require prior written permission for automated collection. [Skift Research sharing guidance](https://info.skift.com/knowledge/how-can-i-share-what-ive-learned-from-reports) discusses attributed sharing of selected insights with colleagues and associates, not automated syndication. The [research publication calendar](https://research.skift.com/skift-research-report-calendar/) lists planned titles subject to change. No public research-report or press-note feed carrying display permission was located. General travel news was excluded.
- **JLL APAC hotels.** The [official RSS directory](https://ir.jll.com/resources/rss/default.aspx) invites reader subscriptions for investor-relations press releases, events, presentations and SEC filings. It is not a research channel for APAC hotels and does not offer third-party public display rights for such a channel. The [APAC hotel sector research menu](https://research.jllapsites.com/sectors/hotels) is a different resource, subject to the [research site's use conditions](https://research.jllapsites.com/terms-of-use-2). No JLL-linked APAC hotels feed with display permission was located.

No guessed feed addresses, third-party feeds, news-feed filtering, copies of articles or reports, or changes to existing source review files.
